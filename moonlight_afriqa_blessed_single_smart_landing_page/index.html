Following the viral phenomenon of SASEZOLA, Moonlight Afriqa (christened by tastemakers as The Magic Pen) retreated into isolation in Lagos and London to manifest his most candid spiritual testimony.

"Blessed" is neither accidental nor fleeting. It marries polyrhythmic Afro-fusion percussions with lush soul guitar voicings, documenting survival, stubborn hope, and the divine grace that converts underground hardship into global anthems.
