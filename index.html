Tales.

Known as one of the most prominent “magic pens” in the contemporary Afrobeats landscape, Moonlight Afriqa (born Imaekhai Richard Igebina) is a fast-rising Nigerian singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist whose sound seamlessly bridges Afrobeats, Afro-fusion, reggae, and alté. His stage moniker is deeply rooted in his native tongue, where “Uki” translates to “Moonlight”—symbolizing illumination, clarity, and raw artistic truth. With over 646,000 monthly listeners on Spotify and his breakout anthem “SASEZOLA” amassing over 7.1 million streams, Moonlight continues to enchant global audiences with poignant storytelling, infectious melodies, and an undeniable stage presence as he prepares to unveil his anticipated new single “Blessed.”
