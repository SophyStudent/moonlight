ARTISTIC STATEMENT

THE STORY BEHIND "BLESSED"

"Blessed isn't just a song about joy—it's about surviving everything that was supposed to break you and realizing your spirit was invincible from the start."

— Moonlight Afriqa

Written during a pivotal transition between West Africa and the UK, "Blessed" bridges organic Yoruba vocal cadence, soulful saxophone motifs, and hard-knocking contemporary Afrobeats percussion. It serves as the lead anthem for the upcoming worldwide studio project.
